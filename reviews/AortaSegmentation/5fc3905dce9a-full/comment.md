- [`AortaSegmentation/AortaSegmentation.py:240-248`](https://github.com/esranko1/SlicerAortaSegmentation/blob/5fc3905dce9a6c55ef314b05ddaba41b2cd83870/AortaSegmentation/AortaSegmentation.py#L240-L248): The Apply handler calls this loop synchronously.
